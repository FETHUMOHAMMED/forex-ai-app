# RISK MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL

## Position Sizing
- Risk per trade: 0.5% (never exceed 1.0%)
- Calculate based on SL distance
- Round down to nearest 0.01 lots

## Drawdown Limits
- Normal: 5% (expected)
- Warning level: 8% (reduce size)
- Critical level: 12% (stop trading, review)
- Maximum: 15% (halt system)

## Daily Loss Limits
- Daily: -2% (stop for the day)
- Weekly: -4% (stop for the week)
- Monthly: -6% (review strategy)

## Losing Streak Protocol
- 3 consecutive losses: Reduce size by 25%
- 5 consecutive losses: Reduce size by 50%
- 7 consecutive losses: Stop trading for 1 week
- 10 consecutive losses: Full review required

## Position Limits
- Maximum 1 open position
- Maximum 3 trades per week
- No pyramiding

## Recovery Protocol
After 5% drawdown:
1. Reduce risk to 0.25%
2. Wait for 2 consecutive wins
3. Return to 0.5% risk

After 10% drawdown:
1. Stop trading
2. Review last 20 trades
3. Check if strategy still valid
4. Resume with 0.25% if confirmed
